3. //With external oscillator 16MHZ. a 4x PLL and divider by 2, what is the duration of a MOV instruction knowing that it takes
   //2 clock cycle?
16MHZ * 4 = 64MHZ
64MHZ / 2 = 32MHZ
1 clock cycle = 1/32MHZ = 31.25 ns
2 clock cycle = 2*32.25 = 62.5 ns
(b) 62.5 ns

4. //what do you know about DC, stepper and servo motors?
DC motors are two wire continous rotation motors used in cooling fans or radio controlled car wheels.
Servo motors are 3 wire motors, they operate like a DC motor but with more precision in controlling its position, can be proggrammed
to rotate by angle, and rotate speed and direction can be changed.
Stepper motors consist of 4 or 6 windings, each controlling a phase of motion, when power is supplied to a phase it spins in that
phase. :)
