Agile is a model that has rules that force modules to communicate with each other.
Scrum is a subfield of Agile that performs these rules in a specific sequence and it's the most popular subfield.

Explain in details what Scrum is:
• Scrum is an Agile process that allows us to focus on delivering the highest business value in the shortest time.
• It allows us to rapidly and repeatidly inspect actual working software (every two weeks to one month).
• The business sets the priorities, teams self-organize to determine the best way to deliver the highest priority features.
• Every two weeks to a month anyone can see real working software and decide to release it as is or continue to enhance it for another sprint.

Explain the Agile-Scrum methodology:
-In Scrum, the project is divided into sprints, each sprint has a specified timeline (2 weeks to a month), here the user stories are
split into different modules, end result of every sprint should be potentially shippable product.
-The steps of a sprint consist of the following:
1. The product owner sits with the customer and obtains his requirements, these requirements are then converted into items using EPIC
2. EPIC is a cutsom tool included within JIRA which we'll be using this lecture.
3. Next phase is sprint planning which decides the priority and order of each item or EPIC
4. The items within every EPIC taken will be seperated into sprint backlogs containing "User story" which includes everything the user said about that item.
5. Then the sprint begins which takes between 1 to 3 weeks, within that period there must be a @Daily_Scrum_Meating within which every
   concern or issue brought up during the sprint is discussed, as well as every task that's successfully performed.
6. After that a potentially shippable product is put out and will be decided by the owner whether to ship it or not.
7. Then a sprint review happens between all people within that sprint to determine how things could've been handled better.

What are roles in the Agile Scrum methodology?:
1. Owner of the product: Full time or part time business oriented person who gathers the requirements from the customer and puts them into backlogs.
2. Scrum Master : Full time or part time person which governs and monitors the scrum system including the developers working on the scrum.
3. Development team : Full time specialists ranging from 3 to 9 people and are tasked to perform the tasks.

When do we use Agile Scrum Methodology?:
• When the client is not clear on requirements.
• The client expects quick releases
• The client doesn't give all his requirements at once.
